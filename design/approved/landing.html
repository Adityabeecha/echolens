We'd rather say “not sure” than guess.

Every answer passes the same honesty checks, written in code — not in a prompt. When the proof isn't there, EchoLens says so.

EVERY CLAIM CITED
A sentence without proof is removed from the answer and shown separately as a hunch.
TWO WITNESSES
One kind of source is only a hunch, capped at 75% sure. Confirming needs two independent kinds.
CAUSE CAME FIRST
A release can't be blamed for complaints that started before it shipped.
TRIED TO DISPROVE IT
Before confirming, EchoLens looks for evidence against its own answer.
