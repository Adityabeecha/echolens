# QUALITY BAR — Evidence Docket

- Thesis: a living forensic case file, not a generic analytics dashboard.
- Palette: mineral paper `#f3f0e9`, graphite `#1d2325`, vermilion `#c53e33`, cobalt `#245da8`, restrained semantic green/amber/red.
- Materials: archival paper texture, exact one-pixel rules, ink-like typography, docket numbers, evidence stamps and ruled registers.
- Type: Fraunces for editorial headings; IBM Plex Sans for readable operations; IBM Plex Mono only for identifiers, measurements and machine states.
- First viewport: left graphite navigation, horizontal signal-to-fix rail, dominant intake register, right running-investigation stack, resolution register below.
- Signature: the evidence thread preserves provenance across signal, hypothesis, evidence, finding and fix.
- Quality floor: no gradients, glow, glass, generic bento layout, decorative charts, fake claims, hidden system state or ambiguous recovery.
- Honest risk: the dossier language can become theatrical; operational scan speed and accessibility always win.
